## Recommendation
**Build (hackathon MVP), conditional.** Medication reminders are an established category, so reminders are *not* the differentiator. The opening is: (1) an honest event record that separates reminder-sent from dose-taken, (2) patient-controlled, revocable caregiver scopes, (3) evidence-backed descriptive insights. **One-line reason to switch:** "It shows exactly what you recorded, why a number is what it is, and lets you decide who sees it."
